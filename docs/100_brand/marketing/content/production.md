# Content production

This document owns reusable production practice, craft development, filming and editing preparation, and production checklists. Publication cadence and launch gates live in the [Content operating plan](operating-plan.md). Asset semantics live in [Assets](../../assets/README.md).

## Production grammar

Use one cinematic, documentary production grammar across Build and Offline material. YouTube is horizontal-first. Capture opportunistic vertical material for short-form reuse without restaging candid moments solely for another aspect ratio. Talking-head material supports the story rather than becoming a separate recurring format.

Use DaVinci Resolve for editing, color, and Fairlight audio. Avoid the teal-green grade. Treat documentary restraint, real footage, memory-not-photoshoot, and observation-over-preaching as production constraints.

## Batching and file practice

- Shoot three to five times more raw material than the expected final duration.
- Treat travel as a multi-episode capture opportunity.
- Use one documented folder structure, naming convention, proxy workflow, and backup process per shoot.
- Plan cross-format reuse so one shoot can support YouTube, Instagram, X stills, and LinkedIn images without forcing every moment into every format.
- Represent every required visual or audio beat through the ontology-defined Production Dependency State.

## Urgent learning agenda

### Cinematic short-form craft

- Study Daniel Dalen and Oscar Lindhardt frame by frame as internal production references.
- Replicate one sequence as an editing exercise.
- Complete four Instagram Reels before the first batch-shoot trip.
- Use Resolve's color and Fairlight tools as deliberate craft practice.

Target: edit a 10 to 15 minute cinematic vlog in approximately 15 to 20 hours by the end of Phase 0 main rather than 40 or more hours.

### Production system

Document file organization, proxy generation, naming, backup, capture ingestion, edit, review, and export. The production manual is considered usable only after Daniel has executed it end to end twice.

### On-camera practice

Record five private minutes daily. The goal is comfort, not publication. Before the first trip, deliver a 90-second voiceover in one or two takes.

## Deferred learning

- Broaden Life Sciences operator literacy through primary industry material and operator observation.
- Develop platform-native writing through actual Publications and review.
- Review editorial restraint weekly beginning in Phase 1.
- Keep production-process meta-content backstage unless later evidence demonstrates honest audience value.

## Phase 0 production checklist

- [ ] Practice DaVinci Resolve for at least 30 minutes daily until import, cut, basic color, audio, and export are comfortable.
- [ ] Complete the daily five-minute private on-camera practice.
- [ ] Study two Daniel Dalen and two Oscar Lindhardt videos frame by frame with notes.
- [ ] Replicate one reference sequence in Resolve.
- [ ] Draft the first-trip shot list from the long-form Script capture checklists.
- [ ] Verify camera, variable ND, microphone, batteries, cards, lens care, and backup supplies.
- [ ] Write and test the file-organization, ingest, proxy, backup, and export process.

Production learning creates reusable Knowledge only when captured through the normal Source and Knowledge workflows. Practice activity alone is not automatically a Publication idea.
