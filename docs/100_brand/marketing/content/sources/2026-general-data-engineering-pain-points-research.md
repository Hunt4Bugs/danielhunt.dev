# General 2026 data engineering pain-point research

## Provenance

A second batch of public web searches, run 2026-08-27 in response to a request for more general (non-pharma) data engineering Topics, covering three clusters:

1. **Burnout and on-call.** DataKitchen, TDWI, ideas2it, and dev.to writeups on data-engineer burnout rates, alert fatigue as a design failure, and unplanned 2am/weekend work.
2. **Hiring and interviews.** DataDriven's "AI Broke Data Engineering Interviews" series, ARDURA Consulting on the junior-developer hiring drop, and SeattleDataGuy's newsletter on junior hiring practice.
3. **Architecture and tool fatigue.** Medium, DZone, Addepto, and dataforest.ai writeups on data mesh vs. lakehouse consolidation, Gartner's over-engineering/budget-overrun figures, and IBM's "modern data stack fatigue" framing.

## Evidence boundary / verification limits

Burnout percentages (e.g. 97% burnout, 70% likely to leave), hiring-drop figures, and budget-overrun statistics are third-party claims relayed through search snippets, not independently verified. Treat them as attributed industry claims, not Brand-verified facts or Daniel's own measured results.
