# Data engineer burnout is structural, not personal

## Premise

The discourse around data-engineer burnout mostly lands on individual fixes — better time management, more resilience, therapy-speak. The more useful frame is structural: on-call engineers don't burn out from hours worked, they burn out from noise, and alert fatigue is a design failure in the system, not a character flaw in the person paging through it. This is a genuine Build/Offline bridge: it's a systems observation about the work (Build) made in service of staying a whole person doing it (Offline).

## Strategic fit

This is the strongest fit in the batch for `Serious Work, Human Plot` — it is the brand's core toggle claim (presence and body as part of the build, not the cost of it) applied to a concrete, widely-felt data engineering pain point, without needing a Life Sciences frame at all.
