# The junior data engineer hiring crisis is a self-inflicted wound

## Premise

Junior data-engineering postings have fallen sharply while senior postings keep growing — and the gap isn't only AI eating entry-level tasks, it's that companies are running mid/senior evaluation bars (system design, distributed-systems experience) against candidates who have never had the chance to build that experience. The system is filtering out the people it needs in five years to save cost today.

## Strategic fit

General operator material inside Strategy's 20% band. A systems-level observation about a hiring pipeline eating its own future talent, distinct from — and intentionally not routed through — the Life Sciences credentialism angle already covered by the brand-level contrarian.
