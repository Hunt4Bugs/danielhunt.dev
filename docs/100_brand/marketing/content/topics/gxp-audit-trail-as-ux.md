# GxP audit trails can be engineered as UX, not bolted on as paperwork

## Premise

Audit trails and 21 CFR Part 11 compliance get treated as paperwork bolted onto a system after the fact. They can instead be designed as a first-class part of the product — the product-level contrarian ("modern web-quality UX inside a regulated stack is engineerable") made concrete through one unglamorous, specific mechanism most generic "AI in pharma" content skips entirely.

## Strategic fit

Direct articulation of the brand's product-level contrarian using a mechanism (audit trails, continuous lineage) rather than an abstraction. Reinforces "boring and serious beats trendy and loud" from the Differentiation Breakdown.
