# Ownership, not contracts, is what's actually broken

## Premise

Data contracts are trending as the 2026 fix-of-the-moment, but the research underneath them points somewhere else: a large share of data engineers cite unclear ownership as their actual pain point. A schema-enforcement tool doesn't fix an organizational gap. This is a contrarian, general-audience angle that questions the trend rather than riding it.

## Strategic fit

General operator material inside Strategy's 20% band. Expresses the brand's "systems and observation over generic advice" posture and its explicit skepticism of trend-chasing tooling fixes, without touching Life Sciences directly.

## Credibility boundary

The unclear-ownership statistic and the data-contracts-trend framing are external research claims and must be presented as industry commentary. Daniel's own ownership-model opinion is a synthesis and point of view, not a measured finding, and should read as such rather than as authoritative research.
