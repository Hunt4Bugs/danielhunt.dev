# AI broke the data engineering interview and nobody's replaced it yet

## Premise

Most companies ban AI tools in interviews; most candidates use them anyway. The old signal (can you write this SQL from scratch, live, on a whiteboard) is gone, and nothing coherent has replaced it — loops have gotten longer (5 to 7 rounds) instead of better. This is a live, high-tension observation about a system that visibly no longer works, aimed squarely at the (B2) audience currently living through it.
