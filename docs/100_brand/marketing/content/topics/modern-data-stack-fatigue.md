# Most data platforms are over-engineered for problems they don't have yet

## Premise

A large share of data-infrastructure projects blow their budget, and a recurring root cause is building for scale, flexibility, or governance the team doesn't actually need yet. "Modern data stack fatigue" — too many specialized tools stitched together — is the visible symptom; premature architecture is the disease. This generalizes the same pattern Daniel has lived firsthand in a narrower, pharma-specific form (S1: "integration pipelines are overkill"), stated here at the level any data team can recognize.

## Strategic fit

Systems and Observation: a model of why data platforms sprawl, not a listicle of tools. Directly reusable general-audience version of a pattern Daniel already holds firsthand evidence for, which strengthens rather than dilutes the claim.
