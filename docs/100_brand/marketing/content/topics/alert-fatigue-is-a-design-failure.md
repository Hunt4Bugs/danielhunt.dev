# Alert fatigue is a design failure, not a personality problem

## Premise

Most teams respond to paging fatigue by asking engineers to be more resilient. The actual fix is almost always upstream: too many low-signal alerts, no severity tiering, no escalation logic that respects a human's sleep. This is a Systems and Observation topic — a model of why the pager keeps going off, not generic "reduce stress" advice.

## Strategic fit

Pure systems-level observation about how data platforms are actually operated, independent of any single domain. Reinforces the brand's posture of understanding work through models and close observation rather than generic advice.
