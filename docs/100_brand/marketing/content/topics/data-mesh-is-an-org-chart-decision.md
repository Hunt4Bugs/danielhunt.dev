# Data mesh isn't an architecture decision, it's an org chart decision

## Premise

Teams keep evaluating data mesh as a technology purchase — which tool, which platform — when the research is consistent that mesh is primarily an organizational and ownership model, not a storage or processing layer. Teams that buy a mesh-shaped tool without a mesh-shaped org structure end up with the complexity and none of the benefit.

## Strategic fit

Systems and Observation in its clearest form: a model of why a popular architecture pattern keeps under-delivering, aimed at engineers and technical leads currently evaluating it.

## Credibility boundary

The mesh-vs-lakehouse framing and the "organizational model, not platform" distinction come from external research and must be attributed as industry analysis. Daniel has not run a data-mesh migration himself; this stays an observation and synthesis piece, not a case study, unless a firsthand example is added.

## Open questions
