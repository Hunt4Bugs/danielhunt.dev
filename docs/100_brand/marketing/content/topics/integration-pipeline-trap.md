# The integration-pipeline trap: lab data plumbing eats more time than the science

## Premise

Most of the time lost around lab-adjacent software isn't spent on science — it's spent working around legacy systems that were never designed to talk to each other. This is S1 ("integration pipelines are overkill; teams waste enormous time on data plumbing instead of science") stated directly, reinforced by 2026 industry framing of legacy incompatibility as a permanent, structurally accepted layer rather than a solvable bug.

## Strategic fit

Systems and Observation fits precisely: the Topic is a model of how the work actually breaks down (plumbing vs. science), not generic advice. It is the clearest first-person articulation of the Vial abstraction's reason for existing.
