# A multi-site trial is a data-integration problem wearing a lab coat

## Premise

A multi-site clinical study reads like a science problem but functions like a data-integration problem: ten organizations, ten formats, ten consent frameworks, and weeks lost before analysis can even start. The bottleneck is invisible from outside the work, which is exactly the kind of observation that gives the (B2) audience a door they didn't know was there.

## Strategic fit

Sharpens the "underleveraged frontier" pitch with a concrete, non-obvious mechanism a generalist software audience wouldn't already know, without requiring Daniel to claim direct multi-site-trial experience himself.
