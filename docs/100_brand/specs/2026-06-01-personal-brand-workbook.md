# Section 1: Brand Positioning

## §1. Brand Journey Framework

**Canonical home:** `IDENTITY.md` Mission and Outcome anchors; `OPERATING.md` Cadence, Phasing, Learning agenda.

### Desired outcome

Datavial and future Life Sciences SaaS products as the primary income stream. Possible expansion into Legal Tech. The personal brand is the **indirect credibility moat**: name-leak into pharma networks over years so referrals start surfacing Daniel without him pursuing them. Sponsorship and creator-collab outcomes lag the primary by years and are accepted as inbound. Operator/peer network is an ambient compound, not a targeted motion.

### Known for

- **Public (Build half):** *He builds Life Sciences products.*
- **Public (Offline half):** *A tech founder seeking human connection through movement, food, and culture.*
- **Internal:** the SWE walking into life sciences with some experience (the Walking-In Scout, see IDENTITY.md Persona).

### What to do

Surface map and cadence from OPERATING.md. Phase 0 prep → Phase 0 dress rehearsal (June 2026 trip) → Phase 0 main → Phase 1 (YouTube launch with next-trip episode, ~Dec 2026 / Jan 2027) → Phase 2 (LinkedIn active distribution when Datavial has 3 to 5 named customers).

### What to learn

Cinematic short-form production craft (DaVinci Resolve, frame-by-frame study of Lindhardt and Dalen), batching and production systems, on-camera presence. See OPERATING.md Learning agenda for the full list with success bars.

## §4. Brand Prison Test

**Canonical home:** Snapshot only. The Build/Offline toggle in `IDENTITY.md` handles the operational concern.

### Step 1: Dread Test

*If you had to talk about ONE topic publicly for the next 3 years, what topic would slowly make you resent your brand?*

> **Pharma compliance / GxP / regulatory minutiae as a standalone topic.** If the brand collapsed into "the GxP guy," it would burn out within six months. The toggle exists to prevent any single topic from dominating.

### Step 2: Viral Handcuff Check

**N/A.** No posting yet. Documented preventively as Editorial rule in OPERATING.md: *the toggle controls, not the algorithm.*

### Step 3: Core Subjects (1-3)

1. Walking into life sciences software (scout journey, Datavial, incumbents, the frontier).
2. Movement, food, presence, observation (Bourdain-lens, offline half).
3. The systems and rituals that keep both halves alive (sustainability, refusing defaults).

### Step 4: Human Layer Inventory (5)

1. Training / gym discipline.
2. Travel (Thailand rotation + Asia).
3. Food + observation.
4. Refusing phone-as-default; presence as practice.
5. IRL human connection (meeting people, conversation as content).

### Step 5: Prison Test

*If my brand stayed exactly the same for 5 years, I would feel:*

> Very positive. It wouldn't feel like work.

Toggle structure passes. Anti-prison by design.

### Step 6: Reframe

> *I am not building a brand to please the algorithm. I am building a brand that allows me to talk about building and moving while still being a full human.*

## §5. Painful Problems v0

**Canonical home:** `AUDIENCE.md` Painful Problems.

> **v0.** Drawn from Daniel's pre-leave memory plus the (B2) archetype. Validate against at least five peer-founder conversations during Phase 0 before treating as load-bearing.

12 problems in (B2)'s voice, grouped into three blocks.

### Pre-leave / pre-entry

1. "I am shipping consumer SaaS or AI demos for things I do not believe in."
2. "I am watching my employer waste years on bad software and I can't fix it from inside."
3. "I have no biotech credibility. Am I even allowed to enter that world?"
4. "I don't know if regulated or GxP software is actually buildable as a startup, or if the moat is unbreakable."
5. "Every founder story I read is SF tech-bro, consumer, or AI. No one who looks like me is going into life sciences."
6. "I would lose my domain credibility and career trajectory by leaving, and I don't know if it's worth it."

### Building

7. "I can build the software. Selling into pharma procurement is opaque."
8. "I can't tell which compliance or validation burden is real and which is theater."
9. "I am scared this market is too small, too slow, or too political to be venture scale."

### Bridge / offline (justify the offline half)

10. "I am in great places with great people and everyone is still on their phones, including me. Life feels less fun than it used to."
11. "I want to travel and live somewhere new but I'm waiting until I 'make it' first."
12. "I am burning out shipping stuff I don't care about, and I don't know if meaningful work is possible without losing the rest of my life."

## §8. Brand Statement

**Canonical home:** `IDENTITY.md` Brand Statement section.

> *I believe engineers who want work that compounds into the real world should walk into life sciences, not wait for permission from the incumbents.*

Ralston format: `I believe [audience] who want [desire] should [contrarian] not [common belief].`

Passes the dread test. Would still be true in 2030 whether the brand has 6 months or 6 years of journey behind it.

### Related positions (canonical homes in IDENTITY.md Contrarians)

- **Brand-level contrarian:** *Life sciences is the most underleveraged frontier in software. SWEs do not need 20 years inside pharma. They need conviction.*
- **Product-level contrarian:** *GxP does not have to feel like GxP. Modern web-quality UX inside a regulated stack is engineerable.*
- **Bridge:** *You can't build for scientists if you've forgotten how to be a person.*

# Section 2: Content Strategy

## §9. Sustainable Foundation

**Canonical home:** `OPERATING.md` Cadence + Sustainability rules. Workbook-specific specifics live in this snapshot only.

### Step 1: Hours per week

6 to 8 hours per week steady state (X posts inline, IG cutdowns, monthly LinkedIn, occasional editing). Surging to 15 to 25 hours per week during travel batches and the week after.

### Step 2: Energy map

- **Energizing:** observation, writing, the build journal.
- **Draining:** on-camera under deadline, editing under deadline.

Drainers must be minimized, batched, or delegated per OPERATING.md sustainability rules.

### Step 3: Realistic cadence

Already locked in OPERATING.md Cadence table. Phase 0: X 4 to 6 per week, IG weekly Reel + biweekly carousel, LinkedIn 1 to 2 per month. Phase 1+: YouTube monthly (bimonthly fallback documented and acceptable).

### Step 4: Freshness window

Morning and afternoon. Filming and decision-making protected in this window. Evening reserved for low-cognitive work or Datavial focus.

### Step 5: Environment fit

- **Location:** flexible during Phase 0; favored locations are home setup + travel locations during batched shoots.
- **Time of day:** freshness window (morning + afternoon).
- **Energy level:** moderate, not peak. Peak energy is reserved for Datavial.
- **Structure needed:** low to moderate. Writing prompts and a captured Wrapping Paper library handle the structure so a perfect setup is not required.

### Step 6: Commitment sentence

> *For the next 12 to 18 months, I commit to the X and IG cadence in OPERATING.md because it supports Datavial's credibility moat, not because it sounds impressive.*

## §14. Waterfall Method

**Canonical home:** `OPERATING.md` cross-format asset reuse (already documented).

### Pillar content

YouTube cinematic vlog (Phase 1+). Phase 0 substitute: trip footage from June 2026 becomes pillar source for the first IG Reels wave.

### Micro-content extracted

- IG Reels (Phase 1+ cutdowns from YouTube hero; Phase 0 standalone).
- X build-journal fragments and observation notes.
- LinkedIn carousel images and case-study posts (Phase 2 active distribution).
- Eventual newsletter when added (not in current plan).

### Process

1. Pillar episode releases first (YouTube in Phase 1+; trip footage in Phase 0).
2. Within 24 to 48 hours, micro-content scheduled across secondary platforms.
3. Monitor signal, double down on what's working.
4. Repurpose winners.

The "Eye of Sauron" rule from OPERATING.md governs which platform is the focal point at any moment; the waterfall is the distribution mechanism *behind* that focal point.

## §15. Your First Three Videos

**Canonical home:** Snapshot only. Expand at Phase 1 prep.

### Status: Ralston-framework version REJECTED

Ralston §15 asks the brand to write the first three videos in the shape: pick 3 to 5 key moments, extract one lesson per moment, connect each lesson to a viewer pain. **Rejected as educator-coded.** See OPERATING.md Documented brand decisions for the two-reason rationale (violates observation-over-preaching; contradicted by Lindhardt and Dalen reference set).

### Replacement skeleton (Lindhardt and Dalen reference-coded)

All three assume Phase 1 launches with the trip-after-June-2026 episode per OPERATING.md.

**Video 1. Announcement / why.** Lindhardt-coded. Tight, declarative, archival plus b-roll. Establishes who and why. Not biography. An announcement.

Working titles:
- *Tech engineer walks into life sciences.*
- *I left big tech to build for biotech.*
- *Why a software engineer is betting on life sciences.*

**Video 2. Arrival into the launch trip episode.** Dalen-coded. Cinematic arrival arc. Follows the offline-half spine. Series-coded.

Working titles:
- *POV: a week building a life sciences startup in [city].*
- *[Trip city] part 1.*
- *Why I'm shooting now.*

**Video 3. Continuation.** Series-coded. Either another trip beat or a back-home build beat that makes the toggle visible.

Working titles:
- *[Trip city] part 2.*
- *The week I came home and shipped.*
- *Bangkok mornings, Datavial nights.*

### Discipline notes

- **Voice inside the videos is Bourdain**, not Lindhardt or Dalen. Lindhardt and Dalen give structural templates. Bourdain gives tonal grammar. Don't accidentally borrow Lindhardt's earnest conviction tone or Dalen's polished luxury-corporate feel (on the explicit-no list in IDENTITY.md References).
- **No lower-third explainer text, no "5 lessons I learned" captions, no "subscribe for more" CTAs in the first three.** Let the work carry it. CTA discipline can relax in Phase 2 when there is commercial pull to direct.
- **The W3 origin moment can appear in Video 1** as a 10-second beat inside the announcement, not as a setup for a stated lesson. The viewer infers the lesson.
