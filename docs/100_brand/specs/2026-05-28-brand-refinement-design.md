# Brand Refinement Design

**Date:** 2026-05-28
**Method:** Caleb Ralston's reverse-order brand framework (outcome → known for → what to do → what to learn)
**Status:** Approved by Daniel. Pending review of this written spec before implementation plan is authored.

---

## Why this exists

`docs/100_brand/IDENTITY.md` defines the brand's persona, voice, references, and aesthetic. It is strong on the middle layers (Maker-Mover persona, Build/Offline toggle, Bourdain ethos, Adrian Per production grammar). It is under-defined on three things:

1. **The desired outcome.** The Mission section reads as a values statement, not a concrete picture of where the brand ends up.
2. **The public-facing referral sentence.** What someone actually says about Daniel to a third party, in industry-standard language.
3. **The operational plan.** What to do, in what order, at what cadence, with what learning agenda to make the outcome real.

This document applies Ralston's framework to fill those gaps. It is the source of the IDENTITY.md updates and the new operational docs that will follow.

---

## Step 1: outcome

**Position:** hybrid operator-creator.

**Primary outcome.** Datavial and future Life Sciences products are the primary income stream. Possible future expansion into Legal Tech. Datavial wins via traditional B2B GTM (go-to-market motion: outbound, design partners, conferences, referrals). The personal brand is not the GTM.

**Brand role for the primary outcome.** The brand is the **indirect credibility moat**. The hoped-for effect is that over time Daniel's name leaks into pharma industry networks through name recognition built by the content brand, so when someone in pharma is asking around about software vendors or operators, his name comes up. This is a slow compound, not a pipeline.

**Secondary outcome.** Sponsorships from travel, lifestyle, training, and gear brands. Expected to lag the primary outcome by several years. Bourdain-style observational depth converts more slowly to brand deals than cinematic-lifestyle does, but it preserves the brand integrity. Trade-off is accepted explicitly.

**Tertiary outcomes.** Operator/peer network (ambient compound, not a targeted motion) and creator collabs (passion-driven, part of how Daniel wants to live).

**What the brand is not for.**

- Not for Datavial pipeline. Datavial sells through its own GTM.
- Not for fast audience growth.
- Not for podcasting, trade-publication writing, or conferences as content channels (those serve outcome A through different motions, deferred indefinitely).

## Step 2: known for

### Build half

> **He builds Life Sciences products.**

Owns the Life Sciences software operator slot. Narrow, recognizable, expandable later. Datavial is the proof. Future products extend it. Possibly grows to "Life Sciences and Legal Tech" if that vertical materializes.

### Offline half

> **A tech founder seeking human connection through movement, food, and culture.**

Mirrors the existing IDENTITY.md sub-thesis with two changes: *tech builder* becomes *tech founder* (industry-standard vocabulary), and *food* is added explicitly (Daniel cited food as central to the curiosity-driven travel ethos).

### Vocabulary discipline

**Public referral sentences (descriptive, used in bios and third-party references):**

## Step 3: what to do

### Surface map

Four platforms Daniel controls. No external dependencies.

| Platform | Phase 0 role | Phase 1 role | Phase 2 role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **X** | 4 to 6 posts per week. Build-journal fragments, Life Sciences observations, micro-notes. | Same. | Same. |
| **Instagram** | Standalone weekly Reel + biweekly carousel. Practice cinematic short-form discipline. | Reels transition to cutdowns from YouTube hero episodes. Carousels continue. | Same as Phase 1. |
| **LinkedIn** | Presence-only. Profile current. 1 to 2 posts per month. Do not be invisible. | Same as Phase 0. | **Active distribution.** Weekly LinkedIn post tied to Datavial case studies, regulatory navigation, operator-level technical pieces. |
| **YouTube** | Not launched. | Monthly cinematic vlog. Hero artifact. | Same as Phase 1. |

Deliberately excluded:

- Podcasts (no guest appearances, no own podcast)
- Trade publications (Pharmaceutical Engineering, BioPharm International, etc.)
- Conferences (deferred until Datavial GTM specifically demands)

These are revisable. They are excluded now to prevent overload during Phase 0.

### Build-half content topic mix

**80/20 Life Sciences first.**

Eighty percent of build-half content is visibly tagged to Life Sciences: GxP, 21 CFR Part 11, lab ops, regulatory navigation, design partner stories (anonymized), Datavial scars, named industry incumbents (Veeva, Benchling, Dotmatics, LabVantage), the open problems in pharma software.

Twenty percent is general operator material (founder lessons, branding, systems thinking) that can be reused without diluting the Life Sciences signal.

The 80/20 split serves the indirect credibility moat for outcome A. A general operator audience reaches other operators, not pharma-adjacent networks.

### Format

**Cinematic vlog as the hero artifact, everything else as cutdowns or fragments.**

- **YouTube long-form (10 to 20 minutes):** the hero. Weaves Life Sciences work, travel, training, and observation into one episode. Unified visual grammar across both halves. Dalen and Oscar Lindhardt are the structural template. Adrian Per and Kirx Diaz are the production craft reference. Bourdain is the tonal layer.
- **IG Reels:** Phase 0 standalone short-form practicing cinematic discipline. Phase 1+ cutdowns from YouTube.
- **IG Carousels:** written reflection, biweekly.
- **X:** daily-ish fragments, build journal, observations.
- **LinkedIn:** monthly presence in Phase 0 and 1. Active in Phase 2.

Talking-head video dispatches as a separate format are **retired**. They become segments within the vlog when needed, not standalone outputs. This is a change from IDENTITY.md.

### Cadence

Post-launch operating cadence:

| Surface | Cadence |
|---|---|
| YouTube | Monthly |
| Instagram Reel | Weekly |
| Instagram Carousel | Biweekly |
| X | 4 to 6 posts per week |
| LinkedIn | Monthly in Phase 0/1, weekly in Phase 2 |

**Fallback cadence:** when life demands it, YouTube drops to bimonthly. Bimonthly is **not a failure event**. It is the defined backup. This must be named explicitly to prevent monthly-cadence collapse from becoming a brand-quitting event.

### Travel cadence

Twice yearly. Per existing IDENTITY.md: Thailand, plus Japan / Vietnam / etc.

**Next trip: June 23 to July 12, 2026.**

### YouTube launch criteria

**Trigger:** the trip *after* the June/July 2026 trip. Estimated Dec 2026 or Jan 2027.

**Conditions:**

1. Two finished YouTube episodes in reserve (buffer rule).
2. A documented editing workflow Daniel has executed end-to-end at least twice.
3. The launch episode itself is in production.

**If conditions are not met by the next trip:** push launch to the trip after that. No penalty. The plan does not break on a missed launch date.

**Why this trip is not the launch:** four weeks of Phase 0 prep before June 23 is not enough runway to dial in cinematic short-form production craft. The June trip is the **production dress rehearsal**. Heavy shooting, no launch pressure. Footage becomes Phase 0 IG Reels material and a sandbox for editing practice.

### Sustainability rules

1. **Batch shooting around travel weeks.** Each trip produces three to four episodes worth of YouTube footage, not one. Without this discipline, monthly cadence collapses the first time Datavial demands a sprint week.
2. **Two-episode buffer at all times.** When the buffer drops to one, the next episode is a warning signal, not a missed month.
3. **Fallback cadence defined.** Bimonthly is the backup. Explicitly named, not a failure.
4. **Brother's help is bonus, not load-bearing.** Anything that depends on his time as routine input is a risk. He is edit relief on hard episodes, not the workflow.

### Phasing

| Phase | Window | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 0 prep** | May 28 to June 22, 2026 | Open X and IG posting. Practice on-camera 5 min per day. Begin learning the NLE (non-linear editor: video editing software). Daniel already holds a DaVinci Resolve license. Pre-trip planning. |
| **Phase 0 dress rehearsal** | June 23 to July 12, 2026 | First trip. Heavy shooting. No launch pressure. Production sandbox. |
| **Phase 0 main** | July 12 to roughly December 2026 | IG cadence sustained. X cadence sustained. Edit trip footage into Reels series. Develop production craft. LinkedIn profile current with monthly posts. |
| **Phase 1** | Dec 2026 or Jan 2027 onward | YouTube launches with next-trip Episode 1. Monthly YouTube + weekly IG cutdowns + daily X + monthly LinkedIn. |
| **Phase 2** | When Datavial has 3 to 5 named customers worth case-studying | LinkedIn escalates to weekly active distribution. Technical articles. Case studies. Direct industry-network compound for outcome A. |

## Step 4: what to learn

Six disciplines. Three urgent for the next 6 months. Three deferred but on the list.

### Urgent (next 6 months)

**1. Cinematic short-form production craft.**

Camera operation, NLE proficiency, color grading, sound design. Specifically:

- NLE is DaVinci Resolve (license already held). Use its color grading and Fairlight audio post as the craft-stretching tools, not just the timeline.
- Study Dalen and Oscar Lindhardt frame-by-frame. Replicate one of their edits as an exercise.
- Ship four IG Reels before June 23, 2026 as deliberate practice.
- Avoid the teal-green grade. Resolve's color tools make this easy to fall into and easy to avoid if conscious.

Success bar: by end of Phase 0 main (Dec 2026), Daniel can edit a 10 to 15 minute cinematic vlog in 15 to 20 hours rather than 40+.

**2. Content batching and production systems.**

The biggest determinant of whether monthly YouTube survives:

- Shoot 3 to 5x more footage than needed.
- Document file organization, folder structure, proxy workflow, asset naming.
- Travel batching: shoot for 4 episodes per trip.
- Cross-format asset reuse: same shoot feeds YouTube + IG Reels + X frames + LinkedIn images.

Success bar: by end of Phase 0 main, a written production manual specific to Daniel's setup exists.

**3. On-camera presence.**

Talking-head comfort, voiceover delivery, knowing when to disappear from frame:

- Record self daily for 5 minutes. Do not post. Goal is comfort, not output.
- By June 23, deliver a 90-second voiceover in one or two takes.

Success bar: by end of Phase 0 main, Daniel does not flinch on camera and can deliver Bourdain-tonal voiceover on first or second take.

### Deferred but on the list

**4. Life Sciences operator literacy.**

Broader industry fluency beyond Datavial's specific niche. Multi-year arc. Built through subscriptions (Endpoints News, BioPharma Dive, Pharma Manufacturing), reading 3 to 5 pharma industry annual reports, following 30 to 50 Life Sciences operators on LinkedIn.

**5. Platform-native writing.**

X grammar (fragments, threads, conversational) vs LinkedIn grammar (structured hook + thesis + evidence + CTA). Develops naturally through publishing in Phase 0 and 1.

**6. Editorial restraint.**

Holding the brand line against off-brand-but-engagement-positive content. Only matters once Daniel has enough output volume that the temptation is real. Weekly editorial review starting in Phase 1.

## Open questions and risks

**Production craft risk.** Cinematic short-form has a real learning curve. The plan assumes Daniel reaches usable craft by end of Phase 0 main (Dec 2026). If craft lags, Phase 1 launch slips to mid-2027. Plan tolerates this (the launch criterion is conditional, not date-locked).

**Sustainability risk.** Monthly YouTube + weekly IG + daily X + LinkedIn + Datavial is heavy. The fallback cadence (bimonthly YouTube) is the safety valve. The risk is that Daniel does not actually take it when needed and burns out instead.

**Audience-fit risk.** The 80/20 Life Sciences content mix may attract a smaller audience than a generic operator brand. This is the trade-off accepted in Step 2 (depth over reach). Should be revisited at month 6 with honest data: is the audience composition trending toward Life Sciences-adjacent viewers or general SaaS operators?

**Sponsorship risk.** Outcome C (sponsorships) is expected to lag the primary outcome by years. If sponsorship revenue is needed earlier, the brand may face pressure to drift toward Adrian-Per-style cinematic lifestyle (option D from Step 2 Question 2), which converts faster but dilutes the Life Sciences positioning. The plan should not change to chase sponsorship revenue unless explicitly re-deciding the outcome ranking.

**Trip-dependency risk.** YouTube launch is anchored to the trip after June 2026. If travel cadence slips (life event, Datavial demand), launch slips with it. Acceptable trade-off but should be tracked.

If at month 6 the urgent learning items have not moved meaningfully, the plan needs revision rather than redoubled effort.

## Editorial discipline rules (carry-over from IDENTITY.md)

These are not new. They are restated here so the spec is self-contained.

- Memory, not photoshoot.
- Scars, not wounds.
- Observation, not preaching.
- Human / lived / curious wins over polished / aspirational.
- No em-dashes in brand copy. Use whitespace, mid-dots, asterisms, or numbered sections.
- Industry-standard vocabulary in public copy. Internal vocabulary stays in design docs.
